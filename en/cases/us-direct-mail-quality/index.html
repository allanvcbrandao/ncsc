Marketing · direct mail (USA)

End-to-end quality for a US direct mail platform

Automation, visual regression and release management for a US direct mail platform integrated with e-commerce, CRM and field service systems.

Industry
Marketing · direct mail (USA)
Period
2024 — 2026

Client anonymized for confidentiality

Context

A direct mail platform in the United States, with a distributed team, frequent releases and integrations with major data platforms.

Challenge

Increase confidence in every release without slowing delivery, on an interface where small visual regressions are costly.

Approach

We structured web automation and visual regression, took over release management and approval, organized the device farm and test case management and investigated root causes through logs and pipelines, with Spec-Driven Development and AI Harness Engineering practices.

Outcomes

  • Automation frameworks and CI/CD pipelines with more coverage and deployment confidence
  • Pixel-perfect visual regression with Playwright and SmartUI, with AI-assisted validation
  • Release management and approval across environments
  • Structured device farm and test case management
  • Testing integrations with e-commerce, CRM and field service platforms
