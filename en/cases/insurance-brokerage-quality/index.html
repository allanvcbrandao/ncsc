Insurance brokerage

Building quality from scratch at an insurance brokerage

Methodology, automation frameworks, quality gates and release governance for an operation that needed to ship with confidence.

Industry
Insurance brokerage
Period
2026 — present

Client anonymized for confidentiality

Context

A growing insurance brokerage with a web product, integrations and frequent releases, without a structured quality practice.

Challenge

Ship new features fast, including with AI assistance, without increasing production risk.

Approach

We designed the quality methodology and the test framework architecture (unit, integration and e2e), built pipeline quality gates, set up release governance and led the adoption of AI in QA with verification gates that require evidence before testing and deployment. We also ran root cause analysis of incidents using logs and observability.

Outcomes

  • Quality methodology and framework built from scratch
  • CI/CD quality gates with evidence-based verification before testing and shipping
  • Pixel-perfect visual regression with Playwright
  • Release governance with validation across environments
  • Adoption of MCP tooling across development and QA workflows
