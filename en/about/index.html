About

About NCSC

Non Conventional Software Company: software engineering with quality from the very first line, without trading the future for shortcuts today.

Engineering beyond the conventional.

Being non conventional means questioning established approaches and looking for solutions that make sense beyond the immediate delivery. At NCSC, quality is not a final stage: it is a principle that guides every decision, from defining requirements to evolving the product.

What we do

We build web, desktop and mobile software and games, bringing architecture, test automation and sound engineering practices into the whole development cycle. We use artificial intelligence to strengthen our processes, while keeping human validation, traceability and clear acceptance criteria.

How we work

We work with companies in Brazil and abroad, whether creating new products, evolving existing systems or strengthening quality and engineering practices.

Our principles

  • 01Engineering firstArchitecture, code and tests designed to evolve and last.
  • 02Quality at the sourcePreventing defects from the start, not just finding them in production.
  • 03AI with governanceArtificial intelligence applied with responsibility, traceability and human validation.

Our mascot

Meet Vladimir

Short legs. Big ideas.

Vladimir is the official NCSC mascot. A dachshund with a curious, determined and somewhat stubborn personality, he represents the company's non conventional spirit.

With his long body, short legs and strong personality, Vladimir shows that you do not need to follow the standard to stand out.

More than a mascot, he is a symbol of creativity, persistence and the freedom to explore new paths.

After all, being different is in our nature.
