Quality strategy and audit

A diagnosis of where quality is costing you, and a test plan proportional to your product risk.

The problem

Bugs reach production, releases are scary and nobody really knows what is covered by tests. Testing more does not always help; testing what matters does.

How we work

We learn the product, the critical flows and the current pipeline. We measure where defects are born and where they are found, and design a test strategy proportional to risk: what to automate, at which level, and what stays exploratory.

What you get

  • Assessment of your current quality process
  • Risk map per critical flow
  • Test strategy per level (unit, integration, e2e)
  • Prioritized improvement roadmap
